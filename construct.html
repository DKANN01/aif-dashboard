Definitions. Target weights are what you type — they do not have to add to 100%, and any shortfall is shown as unallocated cash. Portfolio P/E, EV/Sales and YTD return are weighted by target weight across the positions that report each figure, so they ignore both cash and names with negative earnings. YTD is price-only and excludes dividends. Multiples use the last reported fiscal year unless labelled forward, which is the next-year analyst estimate.
