Definitions. Weight is market value over total account value including cash. 1M and 3M returns compare the current price to the closing price on the nearest trading day on or before one and three calendar months ago; YTD compares to the prior year-end close. Returns are price-only — they exclude dividends. P/E is the last reported fiscal-year multiple; n/m marks negative or distorted earnings. Portfolio return figures are the market-value-weighted average of the equity positions and exclude the cash sleeve.
